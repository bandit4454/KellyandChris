Our story

Every guest travels first class.

Kelly & Chris opened Titanic Tastes with one rule: nobody leaves hungry, and nobody leaves unhappy. The kitchen cooks Texas comfort food with a little ocean-liner polish, and the dining room runs on warm welcomes and remembered names.

Our catering crew brings that same care to your office, your party or your conference ballroom, with every order tracked from our kitchen to your door.
